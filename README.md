# Shadi — For Her Big Day

> **Ultra-Luxury Indian Bridal Fashion E-Commerce Website & Couture Showroom**

An ultra-luxury multi-page Indian bridal fashion e-commerce website designed specifically for the women’s wedding showroom **“Shadi”** (Flagship: Palace Road, Sikar, Rajasthan).

---

## ✨ Features

- **Cinematic Bridal Hero Section**: Auto-rotating carousel with royal Indian brides in heavy zardozi lehengas in Rajasthan palace havelis.
- **Our Collections**: 5 distinct categories with custom circular maroon arrow buttons:
  - *Bridal Lehengas* (For Your Big Day)
  - *Sarees* (Grace in Every Drape)
  - *Indo-Western* (Modern Traditions)
  - *Reception Wear* (Shine Your Way)
  - *Accessories* (Complete Your Look)
- **Editorial Bridal Storytelling**: "Every Bride Has a Story" with styling services, gajra hair details, and appointment booking.
- **Lehenga Listing Page (`/lehengas`)**: Left collapsible filter sidebar (Category, Price slider ₹5,000–₹2,00,000, Color swatches, Fabric checkboxes, Size pills) and 4-column product grid.
- **Product Detail Page (`/product/[id]`)**: Multi-angle 5-thumbnail gallery with hover zoom, live color swatches, size selector, **Size Guide modal**, "ADD TO CART" & "BUY NOW" buttons, trust badges, tabbed specs, and feature icons.
- **Interactive E-Commerce State**:
  - Slide-out **Shopping Bag (Cart Drawer)** with quantity stepper and free delivery meter.
  - Slide-out **Wishlist Drawer** with 1-click move to bag.
  - **Live Search Modal** across all categories and fabrics.
  - **Book an Appointment Modal & Page** (`/book-appointment`).
- **Dark Luxury Footer**: Deep charcoal background (`#161313`) with gold accents, "Because Every Woman Deserves to Feel Extraordinary", quick links, showroom contacts, and "Join Our Wedding Circle" newsletter.

---

## 🎨 Design System

- **Backgrounds**: Ivory Cream (`#FAF7F2`) & Warm Light (`#FDFBF7`)
- **Primary Accent**: Deep Royal Maroon (`#731A2B`)
- **Metallic Accent**: Antique Gold (`#C5A869`)
- **Secondary Highlight**: Soft Blush Rose (`#F5EAE6`)
- **Dark Luxury**: Deep Charcoal (`#161313`)
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Plus Jakarta Sans, Alex Brush
